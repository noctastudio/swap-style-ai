SwapStyle AI Support

Find answers to common questions about searching for songs and generating AI music covers.

How does SwapStyle AI work on mobile?

SwapStyle AI: Song Cover Maker allows you to search for any song on the internet or paste a link directly into the app. There is no need to upload local audio files. Our advanced artificial intelligence then analyzes the track and re-imagines it by applying the characteristics of your selected genre, creating a completely new AI music cover natively on your device.

Can I change the singer's voice in a song?

Yes! SwapStyle AI features advanced vocal controls. Before generating your cover, you can choose to change the original singer's vocals to a Male voice, a Female voice, or let the app choose the best fit with the Auto setting.

Can I control how similar the cover sounds to the original?

Absolutely. You have full control over the output. You can adjust the settings so the new AI cover sounds completely different from the original track, or keep it highly similar while just swapping the genre or vocals.

Is SwapStyle AI free to use?

Yes, SwapStyle AI offers a free version for users to generate short audio previews. To unlock full-length AI song generation, high-fidelity audio downloads, and access to all premium genres, users can upgrade to the Premium plan via in-app purchase.

How do I cancel my SwapStyle AI subscription?

Subscriptions are managed securely by Apple. To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iOS device.
  2. Tap your Apple ID (Your Name) at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select SwapStyle AI from your active list.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.
Why did my AI song generation fail or timeout?

Sometimes, very long tracks or complex links might cause the AI processing engine to timeout. To fix this, please ensure you have a stable internet connection and try processing the link again. If the problem persists, try searching for a different version of the song within the app. For ongoing issues, contact our support team below.
