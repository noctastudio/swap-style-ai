How to Swap a Song's Genre with AI on Mobile

Creating an AI cover or changing the style of your favorite track is fast and easy natively on your device:

  1. Download SwapStyle AI: Song Cover Maker: Available exclusively for iOS on the Apple App Store.
  2. Find Your Track: Simply paste a web link or search the internet directly within the app. No local file uploads needed!
  3. Select Genre & Vocals: Choose from our library of AI styles (e.g., Hip Hop, Lo-Fi, Rock) and select your preferred vocal type (Male, Female, or Auto).
  4. Adjust Similarity & Generate: Decide if you want the cover to sound very similar to the original or completely different, then let the AI generate your new audio track instantly.
